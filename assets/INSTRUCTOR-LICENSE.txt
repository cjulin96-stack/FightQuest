FightQuest — Instrutor 3D
==========================

The instructor model is generated with Ara 3D Gen (MIT-licensed code) from
MakeHuman character assets released under CC0 1.0.

The embedded animation set is intentionally limited to general movement:
Idle, Walk, Run and Jump. The motion-capture clips are derived from the
Carnegie Mellon University Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database
(mocap.cs.cmu.edu), which permits use in commercial and non-commercial
products; the motion data itself may not be resold on its own.
